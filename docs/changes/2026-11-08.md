# Learn graph edits, draft access and bounded recovery — November 8, 2026

This correction preserves the earlier uncommitted Learn changes. It does not
resume a user's generation, start an inference engine, download weights or
qualify P3/P6.

## User-visible changes

- Dragging a block before a prerequisite, deleting a referenced block/stage,
  leaving an empty stage or introducing duplicate ids is refused before the
  graph changes. The learner keeps the existing graph, completion state and
  study chats. Independent moves and unreferenced leaf deletion remain allowed.
- A valid manual graph edit is saved as `modified`, not `verified`. The previous
  verification time is retained for provenance; the previous verdict is not
  inherited. Existing override graphs also display the modified warning.
  Prior audit receipts remain retained and require exact dependencies for reuse.
  A stale/busy editor cannot overwrite the current message. Success is announced
  after the edit's persistence result, not before it.
- A complete draft is readable and exportable while its factual checks are
  pending or incomplete. Editing and completion controls remain disabled.
  Once the audit is stopped, the learner can open **Study draft**; both the room
  heading and tutor request identify the curriculum as unverified. A draft does
  not become a successful audit, and an existing accepted graph stays visible
  while a replacement is being prepared.
- The repetition detector also recognizes dominant repeated sentences inside
  long paragraphs with changing labels. It keeps a bounded sentence window,
  alongside the existing paragraph and periodic-suffix detectors. This is
  repetition evidence, not a time/output-token cutoff or a semantic guarantee
  that every paraphrased loop will be detected.
- Evidence selection ranks all admitted read sources before applying the
  eight-source packet cap. A useful fact after a leading menu, in the ninth
  candidate, or in a brief prose section can contribute to selection. Preparation
  still yields and checks cancellation/ownership between pages.
- Newly generated outline, stage and added-block lists that exceed their
  declared limits are refused for bounded recovery. They are not silently
  shortened. Overlarge effort estimates are likewise refused. Legacy display
  normalization remains compatible; previously discarded text is not invented.

## Resource and verification boundaries

The read-source admission remains 64 sources and 8M characters per audit run.
Temporary per-batch preparation reads at most the first 256,000 characters of
each admitted source, is not persisted and is released after selection. The
wire request still has at most eight sources, 14,400 evidence characters total,
3,600 characters and 24 passages per selected source. The selector is lexical;
it cannot establish semantic entailment or translate arbitrary claims.

The new sentence detector retains at most 128 normalized sentences of 1K
characters per request. Substantial dominant recurrence is required; long
distinct reasoning and occasional reused quotations remain valid. Existing
serial three-attempt recovery, responsive Stop and completed checkpoints remain.

## Regression checks

`make test-roadmap-request` now includes `roadmap_edit_test.mjs`. It executes
production edit preparation and draft admission, checks immutable rejection,
valid moves/deletion, completion/chat preservation and retirement of stale
verification state. Generation checks exercise changing-label cycles at several
stream chunk sizes, long productive reasoning and refusal of every bounded list
class, including manually generated blocks. Evidence checks include the ninth
candidate with a late short factual section and reversed candidate order.

The production browser fixture exercises dependency-breaking drag refusal,
valid independent movement, blocked prerequisite deletion, edited-state reload,
normal Tutor use and explicit draft access. Its missing-evidence branch retains
an incomplete audit while allowing read-only inspection and a labeled draft
study room. Partial-audit Stop/reload/reuse remains a separate branch.

These are synthetic-source and simulated-engine checks. No claim is made about
new real-weight inference quality, complete-roadmap duration or closure of the
remaining Learn source gaps. Earlier failed development/browser attempts remain
in ignored test artifacts rather than being replaced by later passing receipts.

Verified locally: `make test-roadmap-request test-frontend-unit`; normal,
partial-audit and missing-evidence browser scenarios in both Chromium and WebKit
(six passing runs); two further missing-evidence visual captures; `make -j2 all`;
and `scripts/smoke-macos-bundle.sh DStudio.app`. The private read-source replay
