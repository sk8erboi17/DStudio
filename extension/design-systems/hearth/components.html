    DStudio originals / Hearth / v1

    A working visual vocabulary.

    Hearth is a warm house, plainly controlled: plaster and terracotta, a sturdy slab serif, rooms as text tabs, devices as ruled rows with switches that say On or Off, and a dial that states its limits.
