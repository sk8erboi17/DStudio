A letter from the people who make Abaco

“Your shop already has a system. It’s a drawer full of paper.”

Milan, October

Dear shopkeeper,

We built Abaco for the hour after closing — stock to count, invoices to send, a supplier to pay. It does those three things on one screen, in plain words, on the computer you already own.

It is not an enterprise suite. There are no modules to buy and no consultant to call. If something is unclear, you write to us and one of the four of us answers.

Stock
Count shelves with your phone; Abaco tells you what to reorder before you run out.
Invoices
Electronic invoices in the format your accountant expects, numbered and kept for ten years.
Suppliers
Every bill, due date and payment in one ruled list, with reminders that never shout.

Questions people ask before trying it

Can I bring my existing stock list?

Yes. Import a spreadsheet; Abaco shows you what it understood before anything is saved.

What happens to my data if I leave?

You download everything as ordinary files, and we delete our copy within thirty days.

Does it work without a connection?

Counting stock does. Sending invoices needs a connection.
