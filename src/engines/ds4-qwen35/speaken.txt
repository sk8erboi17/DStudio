What if your model could choose how smart it wants to be — for every single token?

Today, Mixture-of-Experts models route every token through the same fixed number of experts. Efficient? Yes. Rigid? Also yes.

Our new inference engine breaks that limit. With dynamic expert expansion, the network decides, token by token, how much compute to activate: from 3 billion active parameters per token up to more than 4 billion — exactly when the question gets hard.

The results? On the MMLU-Pro benchmark, a 6-bit quantized 35B MoE model climbs from 80.5% to about 82.9% — a +2.4 point leap. That brings a compact quantized model remarkably close to its full-precision original, and puts it in range of much larger dense models — the 27-billion class — in pure accuracy.

All of this on a single consumer Mac, no data center required. Same model. Same memory. A smarter router.

The expert count is no longer fixed. It's a decision.

The next generation of local inference is here.
